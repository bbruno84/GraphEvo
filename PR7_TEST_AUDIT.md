## Covered functionality

The suite verifies behavior through assertions:

- SQLite and in-memory opening, path resolution, registry, and concurrent opens;
- rejection of incompatible or unreadable stores without changing their path or name;
- metadata, versioning, backups, and the migration ledger;
- migration lifecycle, including pre-existing completion, fallback, skip, error, and test isolation of global state;
- secure serialization of primitive values, `Date`, `Data`, `URL`, images, PDFs, and Codable wrappers;
- searches, compound predicates, relationships, actions, tags, groups, and deduplication;
- local watchers and simulated remote changes;
- local/simulated Persistent History, tokens, corruption, author filtering, and token advancement;
- file utilities, classification, paths, and the compatible `NSPersistentContainer` initializer.

## Intentionally excluded coverage

Uncovered lines were not forced through tests without diagnostic value:

- real CloudKit paths, including synchronization, import/export, and account/entitlement-dependent fallback; these require a physical device and a configured CloudKit environment;
- `Coordinator` and `Managed*` parts retained solely for compatibility with the old ubiquitous stack;
- diagnostics that produce only textual output, such as `DeepPropertiesScan`, without a structured result to verify;
- internal support code unreachable from the modern flow or generated by Core Data.

These exclusions are scope decisions, not failed tests or missing tests for local
production functionality.

## Completion criterion

PR7 is complete for the local/simulatable scope when the suite remains green and
stable and every remaining uncovered line belongs to one of the categories above.
Real CloudKit tests remain to be run separately on a device.
