# V01 T6 — owner-only share creation remained pending

Status: **inconclusive**. On the physical iPhone, the first native
`share(_:to:completion:)` stage retained no success or error callback result during
the bounded clean observation. Neither a second share nor an independent reader
was reached. This is not evidence of native rejection or successful isolation.
Times below are UTC; the final observation occurred on October 24 in Europe/Rome.

See the [T6 procedure](../graphevo-2-v01-procedure.md), following
[T5's unified recovery](v01-t5-2026-10-23.md).

## Documented capability and tested configuration

Apple explicitly documents that passing a nil existing share creates a new
shared record zone without participants, including related objects through deep
traversal. This makes owner-only shares a supported API request; it does not
establish the outcome of this particular runtime experiment.
[Apple share API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coredata/nspersistentcloudkitcontainer/sharemanagedobjects%3Atoshare%3Acompletion%3A).

The trial used one `NSPersistentCloudKitContainer`, one fresh private SQLite,
the same authorized Development cloud destination and account as the controls,
and direct framework calls with GraphProvider uninitialized. Signed environment,
container entitlements, runtime identifier and source provenance were verified.
The iPhone ran iOS 27.0.1 (24A446). The iPad received the same build but was not
launched for T6. Personal resource/account identifiers remain outside Git.

The model retained the old V01ProbeItem unchanged and added V01T6Member with
optional strings and optional inverse parent/children self-relationships. It
saved two disconnected roots and three children: A-root with A-001/alpha and
A-002/beta; B-root with B-001/gamma. Root values were A and B respectively.
There were no cross-graph or old-probe references, uniqueness constraints,
custom zones, direct CKRecord writes, invitations or participant additions.

The planned sequence was a new share for A-root, explicit Core Data share
persistence, a new share for B-root and its persistence, then 120 seconds of
fresh local reads and an independent fresh iPad reader. Checkpoints precede
each share request and follow successful assignment. Non-owner participants
or public permissions stop the sequence before another share is created.
The 120-second sampling window starts after the share operations; it does not
time out native opening, share creation or share persistence.

## Attempt 1: diagnostic interference

Run `5A91EA61-8678-4E6F-B076-7103EC062F20` started at 21:51:33. The fixture was
saved; initial reads contained all five objects mapped to the ordinary private
zone, without share associations. Completed initial setup/import/export events
were successful. The report checkpoint remained at the request for share A,
with no returned share operation or final timestamp.

After roughly three minutes, diagnostic LLDB attachment attempts failed to
produce a backtrace. The process subsequently disappeared with signal 9.
The attachment/exit sequence confounds this attempt; the exact termination
cause was not established. The crash-log inventory contained the earlier T1
report and no new T6 report explaining the exit. Do not infer a framework
rejection, a watchdog diagnosis or an application deadlock from this evidence.
The incomplete checkpoint and failed diagnostic output were retained separately.
The report checkpoints do not independently trace callback entry before local
callback-result processing, so this evidence does not pinpoint the internal wait.

## Attempt 2: bounded observation without debugger

A single clean repeat used a new run ID,
`2F0594D8-7FDB-4D1F-A1B6-6F9DD6C4FB1C`, and a fresh local store. It started at
21:56:37. No debugger was used in this repeat.

The fixture was saved. The device's report modification timestamp was 21:57:20
for the checkpoint immediately before requesting share A. At 22:02:41, more
than five minutes later, the copied report still showed:

- `fixtureSaved = true` and the retained five-object initial fixture;
- lifecycle `Requesting a new owner-only share for root A`;
- no success or error callback recorded, and `shareOperations = []`;
- no finished timestamp and outcome `Running; not completed evidence`.

The process was still present. After retaining this checkpoint, the coordinator
explicitly terminated it with SIGTERM. Console command exit 1 reflects that
intentional stop. The original JSON was not rewritten into a completed report;
an external summary records the observation limit and interruption.

## Evidence, cleanup and implication

The local GraphEvoLab evidence archive for 2026-10-23-T6 retains both attempts
separately, including their original checkpoints, source/run manifests, signed
build/install logs, console logs, diagnostic attempts and external comparison
summary. Raw account, device and provisioning details remain outside Git.
No wipe, purge, app removal or local store deletion was performed. Synthetic
fixtures and any unconfirmed remote side effects remain retained.

Owner-only share zones remain a candidate for R01, with their documented API
semantics distinguished from this incomplete hardware result. The tested
self-related fixture and call sequence have not established two zones, stable
root/child share membership, cross-device recovery, or selective synchronization.
The next useful investigation is the native share call and its system logs,
not a v2 architecture decision or repeated blind creation attempts.
